05 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find an answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about 7 days, a multilingual site 10–14 days. It also depends on how quickly you send photos and information.

What do you need from me?

A short story about your business, your services and prices, photos and contact details. We do the rest.

Who writes the copy?

We do. We write in plain language, in up to 3 languages, and you review and approve.

Can I change prices and text myself?

Yes. We can add a simple admin panel — or just message us and we update it as part of support.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

The domain is registered in your name so it always stays yours — we help you choose and connect it. Hosting is part of the support plan.

How does payment work?

50% upfront when we start and 50% after the site goes live. Pay by bank transfer or card.
