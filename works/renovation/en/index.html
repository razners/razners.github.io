08 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find an answer? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply within an hour.

How much does the site visit cost?

Nothing. The site visit and estimate are free within Yerevan, even if you decide not to work with us.

Who buys the materials?

Whatever suits you. We can give you an exact shopping list with quantities, or buy and deliver everything ourselves — with receipts and no markup.

Can the price go up during the work?

Not if the scope stays the same. The price and the list of work are in the contract. If you want to add something, we agree the cost first and then do it.

How long does a renovation take?

Roughly: a bathroom takes 10–14 days, a room 1–3 weeks, a turnkey apartment 1.5 to 3 months. The exact schedule is in the contract.

How does payment work?

In stages. You pay only for the stage you have accepted. We don’t take a large upfront payment for labour.

Can I live in the apartment during the renovation?

Yes, if we work room by room. We seal off the work area with film and clean up every day. For a full renovation it is better to move out for a while.
