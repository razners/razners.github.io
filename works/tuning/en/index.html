06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find an answer? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply within an hour.

Will a remap damage my engine?

Not if it stays within the engine’s safety margin. We never push boost or temperatures beyond safe values, and we check everything in the log after flashing.

Can I go back to the factory software?

Yes. We save the original before any work. You can return to stock at any time; it takes about 20 minutes.

How long does it take?

Stage 1 and deletes usually take 1–3 hours, diagnostics 30–60 minutes. Complex cases where the ECU has to come out take up to a day.

Will fuel consumption change?

With normal driving it usually doesn’t rise, and diesels often use 5–10% less. If you use the extra power all the time, consumption will go up.

Which fuel should I use after tuning?

We calibrate for the fuel you actually use. For turbo petrol engines from Stage 1 up we recommend 95 or 98 octane.

What about vehicle inspection after an EGR/DPF delete?

Requirements depend on the inspection type and the car’s year. We explain what changes before we start — the decision is yours.
